the flapper

i ascend to a different realm\
where delirium blooms\
melancholy cascades\
and widowed devotion holds no shame

only at night will you see\
my unabashed pride under powders\
through wistful whispers of reckless desire\
into boozed gardens of hedon

my winged delusions of grandeur\
salute the gilded gates of paramount\
as i marry myself to the lights\
with a pavane for a rural past

as the potions wear off\
i kneel in sequined silence\
to artemis, for health of mother\
and wealth of mine

the clock will not strike twelve again\
until i become who i pretend
