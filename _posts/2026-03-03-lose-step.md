the fear of failure is such a cowardly mindset because not failing ever is the real failure. cause what do you mean you are scared of people's invisible perception of you. dont ever self-censor. nobody is above you, nobody is beneath you. and that's the humble egotism.

you must seek out your own opportunities in order to be great. nobody is ever great hiding behind that cowardly excuse of "I can't" or "I don't have the time to".  you will always have time for the things you prioritize. 

losing is the propeller. losing should only make you more hungry for winning. winning isn't the point, the point of equilibrium is. life isn't about winning or being in the spotlight. it is about keeping the flame lit. and your flame shall be lit for a long time. 

you shall never let any man or company kill the light in you.
